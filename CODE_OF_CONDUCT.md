# Code of conduct

Sendero is a small project about remembrance. Contributions and discussions follow the same spirit as the app:
care, respect and patience.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Respect the cultural tradition the app honours. Changes to imagery, music or messages that touch on the Dia de
  Muertos tradition or on grief are discussed in an issue first.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Never post personal data, photos or camera captures of other people in issues or pull requests.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer at fsantibanez@gmail.com. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who do not follow this code.

This code adapts the intent of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
